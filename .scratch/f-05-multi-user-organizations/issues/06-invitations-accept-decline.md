# 06 — Invitations: accept and decline

**What to build:** An invitee sees the offers addressed to them, from any company, and answers.
Accepting adds a membership without disturbing the ones they already hold — joining a new client must
never cost someone an old one.

**Blocked by:** 05, 03.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An invitee can list the offers addressed to their verified address, across every organization.
- [ ] An offer sent to an address before it had an account is matched once that address registers
      and verifies.
- [ ] Accepting adds a membership at the invitation's role and never moves or removes an existing
      one.
- [ ] An invitee who already belongs to an organization stays working where they were; moving to
      the new tenant remains a deliberate switch.
- [ ] An orgless invitee is working inside the new organization immediately after accepting, with
      no second call and no re-login.
- [ ] Declining records an answer the sender can see, rather than leaving them with silence.
- [ ] Someone previously removed and then invited back returns at the new invitation's role, not
      the role they used to hold.
